# Canada's Carbon Tax and Trucking: The Real Cost Per Mile and How Carriers Are Adapting

![Gas station fuel pumps representing Canadian carbon tax impact on trucking diesel costs](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1527018601619-a508a2be00cd?auto=format&fit=crop&w=600&q=70)

**Published:** October 8, 2024  
**Category:** Industry

As of April 2024, the diesel fuel charge hit CAD$0.2139 per litre, up 23% from 2023. The Canadian Trucking Alliance estimates carbon tax adds roughly $12,000 per truck annually, and that figure is set to double by 2030.
