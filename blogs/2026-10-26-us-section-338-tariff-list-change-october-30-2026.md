# 22 Off, 100 On: The October 30 Tariff Swap and the Loads That Should Wait for Friday

![Keylink Transport trade alert banner: 22 Off, 100 On, with a Keylink trailer being loaded with corrugated boxes and rolled carpet at a dock, the October 30 start date, and the value of goods coming off and going on the US tariff list](../site/assets/section-338-list-swap-2026-banner.png)

**Published:** October 26, 2026  
**Category:** Industry

Washington's October 23 proclamation takes 22 Canadian products off the US 50 percent Section 338 list and adds 100, from carpets and paint to wooden crates and labels, effective 12:01 a.m. Eastern October 30, which is 9:01 p.m. Pacific on Thursday. What comes off, what goes on, and which loads should hurry or wait.
