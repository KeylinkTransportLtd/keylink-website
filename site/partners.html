FAQ

Partner loads,
answered.

Something else on your mind? Call dispatch at 778-666-3626 or send us a message.

Who bills me when Cangaroo Cartage hauls my load?

Keylink Transport does. You book with Keylink and Keylink invoices the load, the same way it bills every Keylink shipment. You do not get a separate bill from Cangaroo Cartage.

How do I track a load hauled by Cangaroo Cartage?

With the same secure Keylink tracking link that comes with your booking confirmation. It shows live GPS, ETA and every milestone from pickup to proof of delivery. No accounts, no passwords, no portal logins.

Who do I talk to if something changes on my load?

Keylink. Keylink handles your billing and tracking, so call 778-666-3626 or reach us through the contact page and we will take it up with Cangaroo Cartage.

What equipment does Cangaroo Cartage run?

5 and 10 ton straight trucks equipped with railgates and tailgates, for dry and temperature-controlled freight. They handle produce, nursery stock, general freight and bulk dry goods, on local deliveries and long haul.

Do Cangaroo Cartage trucks run under Keylink?

No. Cangaroo Cartage owns and operates its own trucks under its own carrier registration. Keylink handles the billing and provides the live ERP tracking link for those loads.
