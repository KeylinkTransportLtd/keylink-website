FAQ

Service questions, answered

More in our full FAQ, or call dispatch at 778-666-3626.

What types of freight can you haul?

General dry freight, perishable goods, refrigerated cargo, fresh and frozen seafood, produce and temperature-sensitive shipments, including time-sensitive deliveries and cargo that needs precise handling.

What is included in the quoted price?

Quotes are all-inclusive: carrier fees, fuel surcharges and standard handling. Anything extra, like after-hours delivery or special handling, is itemized upfront before you commit.

How long is a quote valid?

7 days from the date issued. After that we refresh it quickly, and regular shippers can set up rate agreements for more stability.

Who bills me when Cangaroo Cartage hauls my load?

Keylink Transport does. You book with Keylink and Keylink invoices the load, the same as every Keylink shipment. There is no separate bill from Cangaroo Cartage.

What does tracking show?

Live location, estimated delivery window and milestones, plus live temperature readings for refrigerated cargo. Dispatch tells you early if anything changes.
